How your information will be used

Mission Community and Mission Motorsport will use the information you provide to assess and manage your National Transition Event participation, contact you about your submission, coordinate event arrangements and, where applicable, prepare quotations or invoices.

Please provide information that is accurate and that you are authorised to share. If you include another person’s name or contact details, please make sure they have agreed to you providing them for this purpose.

You can contact the Mission Community team if you need to correct information supplied through these forms or have a question about how it is being used.
