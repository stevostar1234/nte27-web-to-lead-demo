3Additional top-up places

You can request additional staff once for this booking. Check the number before submitting. You can still update names afterwards.

Top-up places cost £50 + VAT per person and are invoiced separately. To correct names after requesting top-up places, enter the same number of places.
The number of names must match the top-up total.
