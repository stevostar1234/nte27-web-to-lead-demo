Join us at NTE27

Join us at The Wing, Silverstone, on Monday 1 March 2027. The National Transition Event is the largest annual gathering of the UK Armed Forces community, delivered by Mission Community and Mission Motorsport.

Incorporating the Troops' Track Day, NTE27 will bring together more than 1,000 Service Leavers, Veterans, Reservists, spouses and partners, family members and Cadets with Armed Forces-friendly companies, government agencies and Armed Forces charities. It is not a typical jobs fair but an opportunity to network, collaborate, and build lasting relationships.

Please read the full Terms and Conditions and fees in the NTE27 Exhibitor Pack. Attendance is at the organisers' discretion. If your application is accepted, you will receive an invoice unless you have been allocated a free space. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice, or before attending if the booking is made within 30 days of the event. Paid space is confirmed only after full payment.
