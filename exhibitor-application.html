Join us at NTE27

NTE27 returns to The Wing at Silverstone on Monday 1 March 2027, following a record-breaking event that welcomed more than 1,200 members of the Armed Forces community, over 200 exhibiting organisations and more than 100 Troops' Track Day supercars.

Delivered by Mission Community and Mission Motorsport, the National Transition Event is the UK’s largest annual gathering of the Armed Forces community. Incorporating the Troops' Track Day, it brings together Service Leavers, Veterans, Reservists, spouses and partners, family members and Cadets with Armed Forces-friendly companies, government agencies and charities.

More than a traditional jobs fair, NTE27 is designed to foster meaningful connections, encourage collaboration and create lasting relationships, while providing valuable insight, shared experiences and access to career opportunities.

Please note: Attendance is subject to the organisers' approval. Unless a complimentary space has been allocated, an invoice will be issued upon booking. Exhibitor space will only be confirmed once full payment has been received.
